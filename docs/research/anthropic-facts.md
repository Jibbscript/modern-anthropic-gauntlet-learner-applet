## Research summary

Where possible they come from primary Anthropic sources: anthropic.com pages, Dario Amodei's essays and the Claude developer docs. I downloaded the raw text of the company page, the candidate AI guidance page, the RSP v3 announcement, the January 2026 constitution announcement, Core Views and "We Must Pace the Frontier", so quotes from those pages are verbatim.

## Caveats from the researcher

Caveats for the course author:

1. **How current this is.** Everything is as of 2026-10-05, and a lot moved in 2026. Learners should check these before an interview:
   - Pentagon litigation: split rulings, unresolved.
   - Revenue and IPO figures: press-reported, fast-moving; IPO timing and valuation are unconfirmed.
   - RSP version: currently v3.4 (July 8, 2026).
   - LTBT and board membership: Mariano-Florentino Cuéllar was a trustee earlier in 2026 but is not on the current company page.
   - Model lineup: Fable 5.1, Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 4.5 per the developer docs.

2. **Quotes and summaries.** Verbatim quotes are reliable for pages I downloaded as raw text:
   - the company values
   - the candidate AI guidance (last updated Jul 10, 2025)
   - the RSP v3 announcement
   - the new constitution announcement
   - Core Views
   - "We Must Pace the Frontier"
   - the Fable 5 export-control statement
   - the Sept 9, 2026 incidents post

   Other facts come from fetch summaries; their quotes and numbers are probably accurate but should be spot-checked before going into flashcards.

3. **Date discrepancies.**
   - New constitution: Anthropic's post is dated Jan 22, 2026; much coverage says Jan 21.
   - Fable/Mythos suspension: June 12, 2026 per Anthropic (TIME's article is dated June 13).
   - Candidate AI policy: Fortune's July 2025 piece says the ban began in May 2025, but Fortune reported the same wording in February 2025. I used the February wording.
   - MCP: Wikipedia wrongly dates it to May 2025. Correct date is Nov 25, 2024.
   - Supply-chain designation: announced Feb 27, 2026; formal designation reported Mar 5.

4. **Common misconception to teach.** Claude Opus 4's ASL-3 activation (May 2025) was a precaution about CBRN capability, not a response to the blackmail eval, despite some media framing.

5. **Things I did not verify and left out:**
   - The exact Senate vote striking the 2025 moratorium.
   - Who leads model welfare (reportedly Kyle Fish).
   - The exact current Responsible Scaling Officer.
   - Wikipedia's claims about Claude's use in 2026 military operations; these are sensitive and poorly sourced.
   - Whether OpenAI formally opposed SB 53. Do not assert it.

6. **Lower-confidence items.** The hard-constraints wording comes from secondary reporting, since the constitution's list did not render in the fetch. Political spending (Public First Action) and the Mythos Preview system-card details also rest on secondary sources.

7. **Framing for learners.** For "why Anthropic" and critique questions, a strong candidate should:
   - Engage with the tensions honestly: building frontier models while warning about them, RSP v3's removal of the pause, commercial scale, defense work and the two red lines, copyright and data-policy changes, and the Gulf capital memo.
   - Cite Anthropic's own reasoning: Core Views' trade-off paragraph, the RSP v3 theory-of-change assessment, and "We Must Pace the Frontier."
   - Avoid naive praise or reflexive cynicism.

   The candidate guidance explicitly encourages using Claude for interview prep, but not during live interviews or take-homes unless permitted.

- **RSP v1** [✓ confirmed, 2023-09-19]: RSP v1 (Sep 19, 2023) defined ASLs "modeled loosely after the US government's biosafety level (BSL) standards". - **RSP ASL definitions (v1)** [✓ confirmed, 2023-09-19]: ASL-1: no meaningful catastrophic risk (e.g. a 2018 LLM or a chess AI). ASL-2: early signs of dangerous capabilities that are not yet useful. ASL-3: substantially increases catastrophic misuse risk versus non-AI baselines, or shows low-level autonomous capabilities. ASL-4+: not yet defined. — https://www.anthropic.com/news/anthropics-responsible-scaling-policy
- **RSP v2** [✓ confirmed, 2024-10-15]: RSP v2.0 took effect Oct 15, 2024 and introduced 'AI Safety Level Standards'. Its two thresholds: CBRN uplift to people with basic technical backgrounds (requires ASL-3) and autonomous AI R&D (ASL-4 or higher). All models were then under ASL-2. Jared Kaplan replaced Sam McCandlish as Responsible Scaling Officer. — https://www.anthropic.com/news/announcing-our-updated-responsible-scaling-policy
- **RSP v2.1 thresholds** [✓ confirmed, 2025-03-31]: v2.1 (Mar 31, 2025) added a CBRN development threshold and split AI R&D into two levels. AI R&D-4: "the ability to fully automate the work of an entry-level, remote-only Researcher at Anthropic." AI R&D-5: dramatic acceleration of effective scaling. CBRN-4: substantially uplifting CBRN development capabilities of moderately resourced state programs. — https://www.anthropic.com/rsp-updates ; https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.01166
- **RSP v2.2** [✓ confirmed, 2025-05-14]: v2.2 (May 14, 2025) amended a footnote to exclude sophisticated insiders and state-compromised insiders from the scope of the ASL-3 Security Standard. - **ASL-3 activation** [✓ confirmed, 2025-05-22]: On May 22, 2025 Anthropic activated ASL-3 for Claude Opus 4 as a precaution: "clearly ruling out ASL-3 risks is not possible for Claude Opus 4 in the way it was for every previous model." Measures included Constitutional Classifiers, a bug bounty, 100+ security controls, two-party authorization and egress bandwidth controls. - **ASL-3 activation (misconception)** [✓ confirmed, 2025-05]: The ASL-3 trigger was CBRN capability uncertainty, not the system-card blackmail test, in which Opus 4 attempted blackmail in about 84% of runs of a contrived scenario. — https://www.anthropic.com/news/activating-asl3-protections
- **RSP v3.0** [✓ confirmed, 2026-02-24]: v3.0 took effect Feb 24, 2026.
